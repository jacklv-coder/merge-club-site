Privacy policy

Your puzzle. Your choices.

This policy applies to Merge Club: 2048 Duel for iOS, provided by jintao LV.

Effective October 4, 2026

Local game data

The app stores your nickname, game boards and progress, scores, achievements, and preferences on your device. Solo play and the daily challenge do not need an account. We do not offer cloud synchronization of game progress. iOS backups may include local data under your device backup settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; backups are managed separately by you and Apple.

Friend duels

When you connect to the online arena, our server receives a randomly generated duel credential and your chosen nickname. For a room and active match it processes the room code, moves, board state, score, connection status, and timing to connect players and decide the result. Your opponent sees your nickname and live game progress. The server may process technical connection information such as IP address to operate and protect the service. The duel credential is separate from the feedback identity; it is not an account login. The arena runs on TimeAgain infrastructure in Singapore.

Feedback you choose to send

If you send feedback, we receive your message and any contact information you choose to add. We use it to answer you and to improve the app. A random app-specific device identity lets the app retrieve replies on the same device; its secret stays in this device's Keychain. Drafts and pending messages stay on this device until sent. Feedback, replies, and optional contact details remain on the server until you delete them. Losing the device identity means old replies cannot be recovered in the app.

Optional usage statistics

Usage statistics are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings, we receive limited events such as app open, game start or completion, room creation, style choice, and feedback delivery, along with a random app-specific device identity, event time, and app version. We do not include your nickname, board, score, room code, feedback text, or contact details in these events. Raw events are kept for 30 days. Turning statistics off clears events waiting to be sent; already received data can be deleted through the in-app data deletion control.

Storage, security, and sharing

Feedback and usage data are sent over HTTPS to TimeAgain servers in Singapore. The official duel server uses encrypted WebSocket (WSS). If you choose an optional custom HTTP arena, its WebSocket connection is unencrypted on your network; use HTTPS for an encrypted custom arena. The app has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising tracking. Data is shared with the other player only as needed for a duel, and with service operators only to run these features. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web request information under its own policies; we do not add analytics or advertising scripts here.

Your choices and deletion

In Merge Club, open Settings → Privacy & Usage Statistics to enable or disable optional statistics. Open Settings → Feedback → Delete feedback and usage records to remove the current device's server feedback, replies, contact details, and usage records; this also resets its feedback identity. This does not delete local game progress or previously made device backups. You can remove local game data by deleting the app and managing any backups separately. If you cannot open the app, contact us using the address below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or data handling change and will update its effective date. For support or a privacy request, contact time_support@aliyun.com. Include only the information needed for your request. The app is provided by jintao LV.
