Help & support

Let's keep playing.

Merge Club is a colorful 2048 puzzle with solo play, a daily challenge, and real-time friend duels. Here are answers to common questions.

Email support

How do I play with a friend?

Both players need an internet connection. One creates a room, chooses a 1, 2, or 3 minute match, and shares the six-character room code. The other enters the code on the Friend Duel screen. Once both players are ready, the host starts the match.

How is a winner decided?

Both players begin with the same board and number sequence. The higher score when the timer ends wins; equal scores are a draw. A match can end early if a player leaves or cannot continue.

Can I play offline?

Solo play and the daily challenge work without an account or internet connection. Friend duels and in-app feedback need a connection.

Where is my progress?

Practice progress, achievements, and settings are stored on this device. Merge Club does not offer cloud sync. Uninstalling the app can remove local progress; an iOS device backup may preserve it.

How do I contact you?

Open Settings → Feedback inside Merge Club to send a private message and read replies on the same device. If the app will not open, email time_support@aliyun.com. Include the app version, device model, and steps to reproduce. Please do not send passwords or room credentials.

How do I delete server data?

In the app, open Settings → Feedback → Delete feedback and usage records. This clears the current device's feedback identity, messages, optional usage events, and any contact details you provided. For a privacy request when you cannot open the app, email support.
